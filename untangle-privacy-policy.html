Untangle for Android

Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: October 4, 2026

Plain-language summary

Untangle is a free, ad-supported puzzle game. The game does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos, or precise location. Game progress and preferences remain on your device. Google AdMob and Firebase process limited device and usage data to provide, measure, and protect advertising, analytics, and optional notifications. An optional one-time Remove Ads purchase is processed through Google Play. The app checks purchase status and saves ad-free ownership on your device so it can also work offline.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Untangle Android application (“Untangle”, “the app”). Untangle is provided by the developer identified on its Google Play listing.

2. Information stored locally

Untangle may store the following information locally on your device so that the game works as expected:

  • the current or most recently unfinished puzzle;
  • point positions, puzzle settings, and selected difficulty or size;
  • onboarding, consent, and privacy-option preferences; and
  • local gameplay statistics, such as completed puzzles; and
  • whether your Remove Ads purchase has been confirmed.

The developer does not receive this gameplay information. You can remove locally stored game data by clearing Untangle’s app storage or uninstalling the app.

3. Google advertising, analytics, and notifications

Untangle uses Google AdMob, Firebase Analytics, and Firebase Cloud Messaging. These services display advertising, provide aggregated app usage information, and deliver game news, announcements, or occasional puzzle reminders. Depending on your device, region, consent choices, and Google’s configuration, these services may automatically collect or receive:

  • your IP address, which may be used to estimate general location;
  • app and ad interactions, such as app launches, sessions, impressions, taps, and video views;
  • diagnostic and performance information, such as launch timing, hangs, and energy usage; and
  • device or app-related identifiers, which may include the Android advertising ID, app set ID, Firebase installation ID, and messaging registration token.

Google may process this information for advertising, ad measurement, analytics, notification delivery, security, and fraud prevention. Data transmitted by the Google SDKs is encrypted in transit. The developer may receive aggregated advertising and analytics reports but does not use those reports to identify individual players.

For more information about Google’s practices, see Google’s Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services .

4. Purchases and ad-free access

Remove Ads is an optional one-time in-app purchase, not a subscription. You can play without purchasing it. Google Play processes the payment under its own terms and privacy policy. Untangle does not receive or store your full payment-card number or bank-account details.

The app receives purchase information from Google Play, including the product identifier, purchase token, purchase state, and acknowledgement status. It uses this information to confirm and acknowledge the purchase, enable ad-free access, restore an existing purchase, and reconcile changes such as refunds or revoked access when Google Play can be reached. Purchase tokens are sent to Google Play to acknowledge completed purchases. Untangle does not operate a separate developer-hosted purchase database.

A confirmed ad-free status is saved locally for offline play. After reinstalling or clearing app storage, use Restore purchases in Help & Settings while connected to Google Play with the account used to buy Remove Ads. Clearing local data does not cancel the purchase or erase Google's transaction records. A pending payment does not grant ad-free access until Google Play confirms it.

Firebase Analytics may automatically collect purchase events, including the product identifier, product name, price and currency, together with app-instance or device identifiers, depending on the enabled Analytics integrations and settings. This information is used to measure purchases and app performance. Analytics collection is not limited to temporary processing in memory; Google may retain it under the applicable service settings and policies. For details, see Google's Analytics data disclosure guidance.

Remove Ads disables in-game advertising while your purchase is recognised. It does not by itself disable Firebase Analytics or optional notifications, or delete data previously processed by Google. Notification permissions and available privacy choices remain separate.

5. Consent and advertising choices

Where required, Untangle uses Google’s User Messaging Platform to request consent or present privacy choices before requesting ads. Based on your choices and applicable law, ads may be personalised, non-personalised, or limited.

When required for your region, the app provides a privacy-options entry point that lets you review or change available consent choices. You can also reset or delete the advertising ID through your Android device’s privacy settings.

6. Permissions and network access

Untangle uses internet and network-state access to request and display ads, submit analytics data, receive notifications, and communicate with Google Play to load prices, complete purchases, and restore or refresh ad-free ownership. On Android 13 and later, the app asks for notification permission before notifications can be shown. Notifications can be disabled in Android settings. The app does not request access to precise location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, phone calls, or text messages.

Puzzle gameplay remains available if an ad cannot be loaded or the device is offline.

7. Data sharing and sale

The developer does not sell personal information. Information described in Sections 3 and 4 is transmitted to or processed by Google in connection with advertising, analytics, notification delivery, and purchases. The developer does not send saved puzzle layouts or node positions to Google.

8. Data retention

Local game information remains on your device until it is replaced, cleared, or the app is uninstalled. Firebase installation information and notification settings may remain until the app is uninstalled or its data is cleared. Information processed by Google is retained under Google’s own policies, applicable service settings, and legal obligations. This includes purchase and payment records that Google may need to retain. Uninstalling Untangle or clearing app storage does not delete information already processed by Google.

9. Children’s privacy

Untangle is a general-audience logic puzzle and is not directed specifically to children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect a child’s name, contact details, or account information. If the app’s intended audience changes, its advertising configuration and this policy will be updated to meet the applicable Google Play Families requirements.

10. International processing

Google may process advertising, analytics, notification, and purchase information on servers located outside your country. Google describes its international data-transfer practices and safeguards in its privacy documentation.

11. Security

Untangle stores game progress, preferences, and cached ad-free ownership locally and relies on platform security controls. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but reasonable measures are used to reduce risk.

12. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when Untangle’s features, advertising configuration, or legal obligations change. The revised version will be posted at this URL with an updated effective date.

13. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Hamid Zarrazvand using the developer support contact shown on Untangle’s Google Play listing.

Untangle does not provide user accounts or maintain a server-side copy of puzzle progress. For local-data deletion, clear the app’s storage or uninstall it. For information held by Google, use the relevant Google privacy and account controls. Contact the developer through the support details above for privacy requests concerning developer-held information.
